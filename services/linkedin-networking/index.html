Services / Strategic Networking

StrategicLinkedInnetworking.

We build a list of the people who matter to you, from investors and buyers to partners and peers, and work it every week: personal connection notes, thoughtful comments on their posts and DM replies drafted for you. No automation tools, no templates, nothing that puts your account at risk.

Posting gets you seen. Networking gets you in the room.

Most LinkedIn advice stops at content. But a post is a broadcast. Deals, hires and invitations usually come from one to one conversations, and those start long before anyone sends a message.

The way it works is simple. The people on your list keep seeing your name in their comments, saying something useful. By the time your connection request or message arrives, it's from someone they already recognise.

What we do every week

  • Build and keep up a target list: investors, buyers, partners, peers and organisers you'd like to know
  • Write personal connection notes, never templates
  • Leave thoughtful comments on their posts, in your voice
  • Draft replies for your DMs so good conversations don't go cold
  • Flag the right moments: a funding round, a new role, a post where you have something real to add
  • Report who's new in your network and which conversations turned into meetings

Why we never use automation

Automation tools send hundreds of identical messages, and people can tell within a line. LinkedIn's user agreement doesn't allow most of them, and accounts that use them get restricted. Your reputation is the whole point of this work, so every note and comment is written by a person who knows your positioning, and you decide how hands-on we get with your inbox.

Is this lead generation?

Not the cold outreach kind. We don't blast sequences at strangers. It's closer to how a well connected friend would introduce you around: fewer messages, much better conversations. For founders selling high value services or raising money, that's the version that actually works. The thinking behind it is in our guide to networking on LinkedIn without being that person.
